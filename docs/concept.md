# Project Concept

AcousticReef is a low-cost bio-acoustic monitoring system designed to
explore underwater acoustic signals associated with coral reef environments.

The project investigates the use of embedded electronics and underwater
acoustic sensing to collect and analyse environmental sound information.

## Motivation

Coral reef ecosystems produce and interact with a complex underwater
soundscape. Changes in this acoustic environment may provide useful
information about reef activity and ecosystem conditions.

AcousticReef explores whether a low-cost, autonomous sensing platform
can be used to capture and analyse these underwater acoustic signals.

## Objectives

- Capture underwater acoustic signals
- Develop a low-cost acoustic sensing platform
- Perform basic signal acquisition and processing
- Record measurements for later analysis
- Explore patterns in underwater soundscapes
- Provide a modular platform for future development

## Development Approach

The project combines:

- Underwater acoustic sensing
- Embedded systems
- Signal acquisition
- Basic digital signal processing
- Data collection and analysis

The system is being developed progressively, with hardware and software
components tested and refined as the prototype develops.

## Current Status

AcousticReef is currently at the prototype and development stage.

The present implementation focuses on establishing basic acoustic signal
acquisition and embedded processing. More advanced sensing, data logging,
signal processing, and environmental testing will be developed in future
stages.
